## The problem

Group plans fall apart in predictable places: nobody owns the next step, costs stay
vague until someone is out of pocket, and decisions stall because there is no structure
for making them together.

PitchIn addresses those three failure points directly — task delegation, expense
sharing, and collaborative decision-making in one place.
