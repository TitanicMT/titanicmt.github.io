About

Structure first. Surface second.

I studied information systems at the University of Toronto, which is where I got interested in the structural half of design: how information gets organised before anyone decides what it looks like.

That order still shapes how I work. Research and architecture come first, interface craft second, and the build is where I check whether the first two were honest.
