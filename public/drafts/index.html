Landing page drafts

Three polished directions, plus two earlier explorations kept for reference. Same content and positioning throughout (“designer who builds”, six curated projects), so the comparison is about the visual system rather than the copy.

A, C and F have been rebuilt against Impeccable's craft floor: kickers removed, unicode glyphs replaced with drawn SVG icons, card radii to 14–16px, single-source elevation, tracking held at -0.04em, and browser surfaces (selection, caret, scrollbar, focus ring, tabular numerals) themed from the palette. D and E are earlier drafts, not carried forward.
