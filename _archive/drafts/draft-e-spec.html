About

Practitioner

I'm Marcus (Zongheng) Tian, a UI/UX designer in Toronto. I studied information systems at the University of Toronto, which is where I got interested in the structural half of design: how information gets organised before anyone decides what it looks like.

I build what I design. Not because every designer should code, but because I keep watching good decisions get lost between the prototype and the production build. Owning both ends is the cheapest way I've found to stop that happening.

Practice
Information architecture, UX research, interface design
Build
